Bubble Atelier Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

What Bubble Atelier stores

Bubble Atelier keeps the photos (including any photo you choose as a wallpaper), notes, titles, categories, statuses, and settings you create. All of it is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and there are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap “Take a photo”, and the picture is saved inside the app. Choosing pictures from your library uses the system photo picker, so the app receives only the pictures you pick. Saving a picture to your library happens only when you ask, and needs only “add” permission.

Sharing

When you share a photo, the original file is handed to the app you choose through the system share sheet. Bubble Atelier does not keep extra copies for sharing.

Deleting

Deleted works, records, and photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then they are removed permanently. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Backups

A backup is a file you create yourself from Settings. It contains your photos and notes, and it is kept wherever you save it. You are in charge of that file; the app never sends it anywhere.

Not a medical record system

Bubble Atelier is a personal craft diary. It is not designed for medical or patient records and makes no claim of compliance with medical data regulations.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on the App Store page. Questions can be sent to the support contact listed on the App Store page.
