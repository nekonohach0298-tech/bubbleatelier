Bubble Atelier

Keep what you make, gently.

Keep your crafts and memories in glossy little bubbles. A gentle making diary, in a sky of your own.

Coming soon to the App Store

Screens shown in Japanese. Work images are sample illustrations.

One work, two ways to view it

Start with just a title. Switch between a photo-focused Work view and a list of making Records. Your work stays one bubble on the home screen.

Move, group, and put away

Hold a bubble and move it anywhere. Put bubbles together to group them, or carry them to the cloud for storage. Bring them back whenever you like.

Look back with the calendar

Tap a day for a popup with photos and notes. Swipe between records from that day, or search and jump to a record’s date.

Make the sky your own

Soft rainbow bubbles, watercolor and pastel wallpapers. Adjust photo size, color strength, and floating motion, with a separate look for each category if you like.

Take photos and notes with you

Export selected works as a ZIP of photos and text to open on a computer. These files are for viewing and keeping, not importing or restoring the app.

Free for up to 10 works

Your work photos and notes stay on your device. No account registration or ads. A single one-time purchase removes the work limit. No subscription.
