Bubble Atelier

Keep what you make, gently.

Each thing you make becomes a floating bubble. A gentle craft diary made of photos and notes.

Coming soon to the App Store

Just photos and notes

Each record is a photo, a note, and an optional short title. The date is added for you. Keep adding records and look back at how a work came along.

See progress at a glance

Change a status and the bubble drifts to its place. Finished bubbles float higher, so one look tells you where things stand.

Move bubbles freely

Press and hold to pick a bubble up. Drop it onto another to make a group, or carry it to the cloud to put it away. Nothing is deleted.

Everything stays on your iPhone

Photos and notes are stored inside the app and never sent anywhere. No accounts, no ads.

Free for up to 10 works

Try everything for free. Want more? A single one-time purchase removes the limit. No subscription.
