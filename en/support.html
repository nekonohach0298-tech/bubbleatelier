Support

Thank you for using Bubble Atelier. Here are answers to common questions and how to reach us.

Where is my data stored?
Work photos and notes are stored in the app’s private area on your iPhone, not sent to developer-operated servers. When you choose to share or export, files go to the destination you select.
Does the cloud sync my works?
No. The cloud is an in-app storage area. It does not upload your works to the internet or sync them with another device.
Can I view my works on a computer?
In Settings, open Export works, select works, and save a ZIP file. Unzip it on a computer to view folders containing photos and text.
Can I import the exported file back into the app?
No. Exported files are for viewing and keeping elsewhere, not restoring or importing into the app. Deleting the app removes its local work data.
Can I get back a work I deleted?
Open Recently Deleted in Settings within 30 days of deletion. Permanently deleted items cannot be restored.
Does Restore purchase bring back my works?
No. It restores the one-time purchase that removes the work limit, not photos or making records. Use the same Apple Account used to purchase, and select Restore purchase on the unlock screen in Settings.
Can I use my own wallpaper?
Open the wallpaper settings under Base settings, then choose your own photo. Turn on Customize appearance in a category’s settings to give that category its own look.
How do I see the guide again?
Open Settings and choose See the guide again in the About section.
