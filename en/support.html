Support

Thank you for using Bubble Atelier. Here are answers to common questions and how to reach us.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Go to Settings → Backup and export everything as a single file. Install the app on the new iPhone and restore from the same screen.
Can I get back a work I deleted?
Yes. Deleted items stay in Settings → Recently Deleted for 30 days, and you can restore them from there.
Can I use my own photo as the wallpaper?
Yes. Go to Settings → Overall display → Wallpaper and choose from “My photo” at the bottom. Each category can have its own.
How do I see the guide again?
Go to Settings → About → “See the guide again”.
